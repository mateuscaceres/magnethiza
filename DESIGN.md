## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a sensory digital sanctuary dedicated to Haute Parfumerie and prestigious Arabian perfumery. The aesthetic is aristocratic, mysterious, and intoxicating—merging the architectural majesty of Arabian palaces with the exacting restraint of Parisian high perfumery salons. It speaks to connoisseurs, collectors, and seekers of rare extracts (oud, amber, taif rose, and precious resins).

The style merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Atmospheric Layering** and subtle **Tactile Metallic Accents**. Deep obsidian grounds the experience, layered with steeped burgundy velvets and glowing threads of imperial gold. UI elements act like museum vitrines or crystal flacons: spare, immaculate, with quiet micro-interactions, liquid transitions, and ambient golden reflections that reward deliberate exploration.

## Typography

The typography couples high-contrast editorial serifs (`Playfair Display`) with clean geometric sans-serif phrasing (`Outfit`). 

- **Display & Headline Tiers:** Rendered in `Playfair Display`. Evokes the master labels of bespoke crystal flacons and perfumers' notebooks. Employs light italic gestures for olfactory notes (e.g., *Extrait de Parfum*, *Grand Cru Oud*).
- **Body & Structural Information:** Rendered in `Outfit` with generous line-heights and featherweight tracking (`300` weight default). This ensures maximum readability against dark obsidian backgrounds without visually crowding the delicate imagery.
- **Micro-Copy & Metadata:** Rendered in all-caps tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.22em`) for batch codes, harvest years, notes pyramids (Top, Heart, Base), and architectural navigation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an haute couture showroom—expansive, rhythmic, and calm. Whitespace (dark void) is treated as an active luxury material.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container of 1380px to preserve intimate visual framing. Scales to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Pacing & Cadence:** Generous vertical sections separated by `space-2xl` allow individual flacons and olfactory stories room to resonate. Dense rows are strictly forbidden.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Desktop outer margins (`4rem`) compress gracefully to `2.5rem` on tablet and `1.25rem` on mobile. Horizontal scroll patterns are adopted on mobile exclusively for olfactory notes and raw ingredient carousels to preserve tall, elegant aspect ratios.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through steeped tonal luminosity and hairline gold containment rather than harsh dropshadows.

- **Obsidian Tiering:**
  - **Tier 0 (Canvas):** Deepest void (`#080708`) used for main background expanses.
  - **Tier 1 (Surface Glass):** Base obsidian (`#0C0A0C` to `#141115`) with ultra-fine borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
  - **Tier 2 (Maceration Chambers):** Infused bordeaux tinting (`#1E0811` to `#2B0D18`), utilized for product vitrines, discovery trays, and cart draws.
- **Ambient Golden Luminescence:** Floating elements (such as quick-view flacon modals or floating concierge bars) use an ambient amber halo: `box-shadow: 0 12px 48px -8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(58, 16, 31, 0.6)`.
- **Low-Contrast Filigree Borders:** Panels are bound by 1px hairline borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` default, brightening to `#D4AF37` on focus or hover).

## Shapes

The design system embraces architectural sharpness (`roundedness: 0`), taking inspiration from emerald-cut crystal flacons, solid brass perfume caps, and lacquered presentation coffrets.

- **Corners:** Crisp, rectilinear 0px corners across primary cards, inputs, and primary CTAs convey formality, permanence, and regal heritage.
- **Exceptions:** Miniature ingredient chips and circular scent blotter swatch indicators may feature perfect circles (`50%` radius), providing a geometric counterpoint to the razor-sharp container frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Imperial CTA:** Solid Imperial Gold (`#D4AF37`) fill with deep obsidian text (`#0C0A0C`), sharp corners, uppercase tracking (`0.18em`). Hover activates a soft amber radiance (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`) and lifts brightness to `#DFB76C`.
- **Secondary Bordeaux Ghost Button:** Obsidian fill with a 1px border of `#3A101F`, text in `#E8E2D5`. On hover, transitions into a wine velvet fill (`#2B0D18`) with an Imperial Gold border (`#D4AF37`).
- **Direct WhatsApp Concierge:** Solid `#25D366` button with `#0C0A0C` bold text, dedicated solely to private bespoke consultations and order assistance. Positioned fixed at the bottom right corner with a subtle radial pulse.

### Flacon Cards (Product Vitrines)
- **Container:** Dark Obsidian (`#141115`) resting on `#080708`, bordered with 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Visual Presentation:** Dramatic product photography with warm golden backlighting. Hover causes an internal radial gradient shift featuring deep wine velvet (`#3A101F` at 25% opacity) radiating behind the bottle.
- **Metadata Tier:** Category label in `label-caps` (`#B8A995`), Fragrance Name in `headline-sm` (`#FBF8F2`), Concentration and Volume in `body-md` (`#E8E2D5`), price emphasized in `#DFB76C`.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Rectilinear `#080708` background with an inset 1px border of `#2B0D18`. Text rendered in `#FBF8F2`.
- **States:** Focus replaces the border with `#D4AF37` and projects an interior ambient glow (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`). Placeholder text set in muted sand (`#B8A995`).

### Fragrance Note Pyramid & Chips
- **Chips / Accord Tags:** Sharply bordered pills or boxes (`border: 1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`), transparent obsidian background, `#E8E2D5` text.
- **Active / Filtered State:** Background fills with steeped wine (`#3A101F`), border elevates to `#DFB76C`, and text sharpens to `#FBF8F2`.

### Olfactory Accord Bars
- Visual strength indicators (e.g., Sillage, Longevity, Oud Intensity) feature ultra-thin 2px rules. The track is rendered in `#1E0811`, with filled active states shimmering in a gradient from `#C5A059` to `#DFB76C`.
